Why Venice Tile?

Three Decades of Precision Stonework Across Northern California

We started in San Jose in June 1987 as trailblazers in the Bay’s marble industry, and expanded throughout Northern California. With over 35 years of experience in the industry, we have established ourselves as experts in fabricating natural stone, quartz, and porcelain slabs.

Our cutting-edge equipment ensures precise and efficient fabrication to meet your unique needs. In addition to our fabrication services, we specialize in installing a variety of surfaces, including tile, pavers, concrete, and hardscape.

“We can also help you create your dream outdoor living space, complete with outdoor kitchens, fire pits, pool coping, artificial grass, pergola, and more. Let us bring your vision to life with our unparalleled expertise and exceptional customer service.”
